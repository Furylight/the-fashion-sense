Frequently Asked Questions

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work?

Simply select Cash on Delivery during checkout. Our courier service delivers the parcel to your doorstep anywhere in Sri Lanka, and you pay cash directly to the courier agent upon receiving your order.

What is your exchange policy if the size does not fit?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free exchange! If your garment doesn't fit quite right, WhatsApp us with your order number. Our rider will drop off the replacement size and pick up the exchange piece simultaneously.

How long will delivery take across Sri Lanka?

Deliveries within the Western Province (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara) take 24–48 hours. Outstation deliveries take 2–3 business days. You will receive tracking SMS updates.
